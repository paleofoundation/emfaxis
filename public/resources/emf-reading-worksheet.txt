EMF AXIS — CLAIM & EXPOSURE READING WORKSHEET
Version 0.2-draft | 8 October 2026 | Free editorial resource

PURPOSE
Turn one public claim into a traceable reading note. This is not a meter,
medical test, exposure-limit table, diagnostic tool or safety certificate.
No field asks for a name, address, diagnosis or personal health history.
No safety verdict can be obtained by adding up these fields.

MISSING-DATA KEY
Not available to me = an access gap.
Not yet checked = work remains.
Not reported in the source I read = the inspected source did not state it.
Never turn any of these into zero, no effect, or safe/unsafe.

REUSE / PRIVACY
Keep a local or printed copy if useful. This worksheet has no sending or
saving service. Do not put sensitive details in a shared copy. The reading
method comes from the EMF Axis draft guides; worked scientific examples
and full references are in first-guide.html. It does not create new evidence.
